III

The Buddha, standing at the edge of the lotus pond in Paradise, had been watching all of this closely, and when Kandata sank like a stone to the bottom of the Pool of Blood, he began to stroll on again with a sorrowful face. Kandata’s heartless wish to escape Hell by himself alone, and the fitting punishment that sent him tumbling back to where he came from, must have seemed shameful in the Buddha’s eyes.

But the lotuses in the lotus pond of Paradise did not care in the least about such things. Their pearl-like white blossoms swayed their cups around the Buddha’s feet, and from the golden stamens at their hearts an indescribably sweet fragrance drifted out ceaselessly all around. It must have been nearly noon in Paradise by now.

(April 16, 1918)
